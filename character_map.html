Sadie Green

My opinion

I love that Sadie is allowed to be difficult, ambitious, wounded, and wildly talented all at once.

Key facts

Game designer, lifelong creative rival, and someone who keeps remaking herself when the world asks too much.

Why they matter

She feels like a reminder that women in stories can want greatness without becoming less lovable.

Notable moments

Every sharp conversation with Sam; every quiet choice to keep making art anyway.

Piranesi

My opinion

He is one of the gentlest narrators I have ever met. His openness makes the mystery ache in the best way.

Key facts

Lives in a vast labyrinthine House, keeps careful journals, and sees beauty where others see emptiness.

Why they matter

He makes wonder feel like a form of resistance, even when someone else has tried to rewrite his life.

Notable moments

The sea moving through the halls; his tenderness toward the bones; the slow return of his own name.

Demon Copperhead

My opinion

Demon’s voice is electric: hilarious, grieving, furious, and far more tender than he lets on.

Key facts

A boy from Appalachia navigating foster care, addiction, friendship, and a world that keeps underestimating him.

Why they matter

His story insists that being seen clearly is not the same thing as being saved — but it can be a beginning.

Notable moments

His first friendships, his fiercely observed humor, and every time he chooses to keep going.

Klara

My opinion

Klara breaks my heart because she observes human devotion with such clarity, then offers it so freely herself.

Key facts

An Artificial Friend powered by sunlight, with a watchful eye and a startling faith in the people she loves.

Why they matter

She turns questions about consciousness and loyalty into something intimate, delicate, and very human.

Notable moments

Her view from the shop window; her bargain with the sun; her patient, unwavering care for Josie.

Martha

My opinion

Martha is proof that a quiet character can hold an entire emotional landscape in the curve of one sentence.

Key facts

A keeper of stories, small rituals, and the sort of practical wisdom that makes a room feel safe.

Why they matter

She reminds me that care is not passive. It is a craft, a choice, and sometimes a brave act.

Notable moments

Her tea-on-the-table steadiness, her honest observations, and the way she notices what everyone else misses.

The best characters keep unfolding.

Some become old friends. Some leave a mark. Some simply hand us a new way to look at the world.
